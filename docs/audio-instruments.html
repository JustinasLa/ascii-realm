Instruments

Nine instruments carried in the off hand. Each has its own synthesised voice: plucked, hammered and bowed strings, a metal tine, free reeds, a chanter and drone, a breathy flute and bright brass. Keys 1-8 play C D E F G A B C; Shift plays the second row.

Notes ring out to their natural length, so you can play a melody. Stop silences everything.
