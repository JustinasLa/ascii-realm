# Controls

## Keyboard and mouse

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| `W A S D` / arrow keys | Walk |
| `Shift` | Run |
| `Space` | Jump; again in the air for a trick (a barrel roll when running) |
| `J` / left click | Strike with the sword (a click turns and cuts towards the cursor; `J` cuts the way you face): repeat for a combo, run for a thrust, jump for a plunge; cuts fell trees and mow tall grass |
| Hold `J` / left click | Charge, then release for a spinning slash |
| `F` | Draw or sheathe the sword |
| `T` | Stow the torch or draw a fresh lit torch |
| `G` | Throw the carried torch in the direction faced; dry ground ignites on landing |
| `C` | Build a campfire on dry ground, or extinguish a nearby fire |
| `E` | Pick up the nearest ground item, or else use a furnace, an alloy forge or a weapon station in reach, or else disturb a nearby glowcap mushroom to release glowing spores (the same as TAKE / SMELT / FORGE / SMITH / SPORES; fires are left to `C`) |
| `R` / right click | Cast the rune bound to `R` towards the cursor; the caster turns to face it |
| Hold middle mouse | Open the rune wheel: point at a school (Oseni, Seithr, Cerrith, Mitlan), then at a rune, and let go to bind it to `R`. Pushing out through a school opens it in one motion; letting go on a school keeps its runes open for a click. Right click or the centre goes back, `Escape` closes |
| `M` | Mute or unmute all sound |
| `N` / Audio panel's Skip song button | Switch to the next pair in the current season and weather playlist |
| Weather & seasons panel's Season | Follow automatic seasons or choose Spring, Summer, Autumn or Winter for music and ambience |
| Weather & seasons panel's Weather / Intensity | Choose Clear or one of 17 weather presets and adjust sound and visual intensity |
| `L` / Weather & seasons panel's Summon lightning button | Summon a nearby lightning strike for testing in any weather |
| Audio panel's Weather slider | Adjust weather volume; Ambience and Master also affect it |
| Game panel's Save now / phone menu's Save game | Save the game now (it also saves every minute and when you leave) |
| Mouse wheel / `+` `-` | Zoom |
| `1`–`8`, instrument in the off hand | Play notes C D E F G A B C |
| `Shift`+`1`–`8`, instrument in the off hand | Play the chords (lute, vielle, celtic harp, dulcimer, kalimba, accordion) or notes 9–16 an octave up (flute, trumpet, bagpipe) |
| `I` / `Tab` | Open or close the inventory (`Escape` also closes it) |
| Left click, with inventory open | On a bag card: equip it (arrows fill the quiver) or use it; on a filled equipment box: take it off. On a filter tab: show only that kind |
| Right click, with inventory open | Drop a bag card's whole stack on the ground |
| Drag, with inventory open | Drag a bag card onto a lit equipment box to equip it there, or onto another card to swap them; drag worn gear into the bag to take it off, or a ring onto the other ring box to swap them. `Escape` cancels a drag |
| Hover, with inventory open | Show the item tooltip for a bag card or a filled equipment box (the arrow keys show the selected one's) |
| `G`, with inventory open | Switch between the bag and equipment |
| Arrow keys, with inventory open | Move through the shown cards, or around the equipment |
| `Enter`, with inventory open | Use or equip the selected bag item, or take off selected gear. Equip the stone pickaxe here, then strike an ore block with it to mine it (the prompt shows what a block needs) |
| `E` / `U`, with inventory open | Equip the selected item / unequip the selected gear |
| `X`, with inventory open | Drop the selected bag stack |
| `C`, with inventory open | Turn to the next page (Inventory, Character, Characters) |
| Arrow keys / `Tab`, with a furnace open | Move through its slots, your satchel's resources and Close |
| `Enter` / `Space`, with a furnace open | Put the chosen resource in, or take a slot's contents back |
| `Escape` / `E`, with a furnace open | Close the furnace |
| Arrows / `Tab`, `Enter` / `Space`, `Escape` / `E`, with an alloy forge open | As with a furnace: move through its buttons, satchel rows and Known alloys (when that list is too long to show, arrows up and down, `Page Up` / `Page Down`, `Home`, `End` and `Space` scroll it), add an ingredient or press a button, and close it |
| `1`–`6` (top row or numpad), with a weapon station open | Strike the piece with Hit, Small Hit, Etch, Whittle, Engrave or Sew |
| Arrows / `Tab`, `Enter` / `Space`, `Escape` / `E`, with a weapon station open | Move through its pieces, hit buttons, Finish, Cancel, Take and satchel rows, choose a piece, add metal or press a button, and close it |
| Typing, then `Enter`, after a discovery | Name the new alloy; in the field the keys type, and only `Tab` and `Escape` leave it |
| `Escape`, in the character creator | Cancel an edit or a new character (a started one asks first); the first character can't be cancelled |
| `Left` / `Right` / `Home` / `End`, on a page tab | Move between the inventory's pages |
| `B` | Enter the Scenery builder, or return to play and close its panel |

## Instruments

With an instrument in the off hand, `1`–`8` (or the numpad's `1`–`8`) play
its notes the moment they go down, and `Shift` held at the press plays the
second layer. Holding a key down plays its note once. What the main hand holds
doesn't matter, and walking, running (`Shift` still runs), striking, jumping
and casting all work while you play.

Note slots beside the Inventory button show the layout while you carry one: slots 1–8 read
`C D E F G A B C` with the exact pitch below, chord names (or the octave
above) while `Shift` is held, and the played slot lights up. The hint reads,
for example, `Lute | 1-8 notes | Shift chords`. Clicking a note slot plays it,
and `Shift`-click plays the second layer.

Notes don't play in the Scenery builder, while typing, with the
inventory, chat or rune wheel open, or while down. A note glyph `d`, coloured
by pitch, pops up 2 m above your head with each note.

Phones can't play instruments yet.

## Chat

In a lobby, press `Enter` to talk. Plain text goes to your channel, which is
local speech (**Say**) until you pick another in the bar's list or with
`/channel`. `/<command> <message>` sends one line on another channel:

| Command | Channel | Reaches |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/rp` | Say: `Name: "message"` | 15 m |
| `/shout` | Shout | 24 m |
| `/yell`, `/y` | Yell | 48 m |
| `/whisper`, `/wh` | Whisper | 2 m |
| `/looc` | Local out-of-character chat: `[LOOC] Name: message` | 20 m |
| `/ooc` | Out-of-character chat: `[OOC] Name: message` | the whole lobby |
| `/me` | Emote: `* Name waves` | 20 m |
| `/scene` | Scene description: `[Scene] Name: *message*` | 20 m |

Names show in each speaker's name colour, except in LOOC and OOC lines, where
they are plain.

Speech (Say, Shout, Yell, Whisper) is heard clearly up to 60 % of its reach
and blurs beyond that: words break into `th...`, lose sounds and trail off,
until a listener near the edge hears only `*muffled voices*`. You always see
your own words as you said them. If nobody else heard a line, the log says
"No one can hear you in this channel."

`/channel` shows your channel and `/channel <name>` switches it.
`/channeltoggle ooc` or `/channeltoggle looc` hides or shows that channel.
`/help` lists the commands. Messages are up to 256 characters of plain text
(accents and emoji are dropped); after a burst of ten, you can send about one a
second. The log keeps the last 100 lines. While the bar is closed it shows the
newest ten for 10 seconds each; open, you can scroll back through them all.
Joins and leaves show as quiet lines. Chat isn't saved, and players who join
later don't see earlier lines.

What is said, shouted or yelled also floats over the speaker's head for
5 seconds, as you heard it: clear, blurred, or `*muffled voices*`. Your own
lines float over your head. Each speaker's last five lines stack in one box,
the oldest on top; a long stack shows only its newest lines (fewer on
phones, and fewer still when the speaker stands near the top of the screen).
Boxes keep clear of a phone's notch and rounded corners. Whispers, emotes, scene lines and out-of-character chat show only in
the log. A bubble over someone standing under a tree is drawn fainter.

## Target

The creature you last landed a blow on, with the sword, an arrow or thrown
spear, or one of your own runes, becomes your target. Its name and health
(`HP 23/35`, with "enraged" when a monster turns furious) show in a target
frame at the top of the screen, and its overhead health bar gets a thin
outline. When one blow strikes several creatures, the target is the one the
touch aim assist turned to, or else the one nearest straight ahead. Wounds
dealt by monsters or other animals never pick a target. Striking another
creature replaces it. When the target dies, the frame shows it as slain for
a moment and then clears; it also clears once the creature is gone from the
world, more than 20 m away, or 15 s have passed without another blow, and
when you are struck down.

The frame sits at the top centre, or beside the player card in narrow
windows. On phones it is a slim strip on the top edge between the HP/MP strip
and the **=** button; on the narrowest portrait phones it sits on a second
row under the HP/MP strip.

## Loot

A monster you helped bring down, with at least 1 % of its health taken by
your own sword or runes, may leave loot on dry ground beside its body, where
you can walk to it: a skeleton always leaves a bone or two, often an arrow or
two, and now and then an outlaw's hood; a goblin or an elf sometimes leaves an
iron ingot or a denar, and a troll sometimes four of either. A monster slain by a friend's rune
alone leaves nothing on your screen. Every stack on the ground, the hunt's
included, has its name shown above it. Stacks within 4 m are always named;
farther ones whose names would overlap share one label ("Bone x2, Arrow x1 +2
more"). Walk close to draw them in, or use E / TAKE. A full bag leaves them
lying with the name dimmed. They last five minutes. A stack dropped from the
bag keeps what makes it its own, such as a weapon's material, and comes back
as it went down, never joining a bag stack that differs.

## Denars

Coins sometimes lie among a slain monster's loot, and felling a tree now and
then turns up a few beside the stump. Each shows its value above it, such as
`0.73d`. Walk over them or use E / TAKE: they go straight to your pouch, shown
under your health as "Pouch 0.73d", even when the bag is full. Each gain shows
a `+0.73d` notice and the pouch line brightens for a moment. The pouch is kept
on this device between visits, and you keep it when you are struck down.

## Touch

On a touch screen, drag the left pad to walk and push it to the outer edge to
run. The other thumb has a cluster in the bottom-right corner:

- **STRIKE**, the large square in the corner, draws the sword on the first
  strike, chains a combo when tapped again and charges a spin when held. The
  sword is put away by itself after a few quiet seconds standing still. With a
  bow or spear, hold STRIKE to draw and lift to shoot or throw. With the stone
  pickaxe equipped from the inventory, STRIKE mines the ore block you face.
- **JUMP** sits to its left.
- **CAST** sits above it. Tap it to cast the bound rune; hold it to open the
  rune wheel on the same finger, slide to a school and lift, then tap a rune
  to bind it to **CAST**. While the wheel is open, a stick already held keeps
  steering, but a new touch goes to the wheel.
- **INTERACT** appears on the diagonal between them only when something is in
  reach: **SPORES** by a ready glowcap, **DOUSE** by a fire (it never builds
  one), **SMELT** by a furnace, **FORGE** by an alloy forge, **SMITH** by a weapon
  station and **TAKE** by a ground item, and it is hidden otherwise. A furnace opens as a full-screen
  sheet: tap a resource to put it in, tap a slot to take it back, and
  **Close** to return to play. An alloy forge opens the same way: tap
  ingredients, then **Fire**; name a new alloy and tap **Rename** or Done. A
  weapon station too: tap a piece, then its metal, then the hit buttons, and
  **Finish**.
- The **inventory** (in the phone menu) is a full-screen sheet that scrolls as
  a whole. Tap a filter tab to show one kind. Tap a bag card to equip or use
  it, and a filled equipment box to take its gear off. Hold a card or a
  filled box to pin its tooltip, with buttons to **equip**, **use** or
  **take off** and to **drop**; tap anywhere else to close it. Hold, then
  move to drag it: the item rides above your finger, the boxes it fits light
  up, and the sheet scrolls near its top and bottom edges.

Strikes, shots and attack runes from these buttons turn to the nearest enemy
within 30° either side of your facing, within the weapon's reach plus a stride,
the bow's or spear's range, or the rune's flight range; with no enemy there
they follow your facing. Heals and wards never turn toward an enemy. Phones
that support it give a short vibration when a blow lands and when you are hurt.
Pinch the scene with two fingers to zoom. Controls support multiple fingers and
reset when a touch is cancelled or the page is backgrounded.

Phones show only the stick, the thumb cluster, a slim HP/MP strip on the top
edge and a small **=** menu button in the top-right corner. The menu opens a
full-screen sheet with **Inventory**, **Save game**, **Multiplayer** and **Audio**; **Close**
returns to a clean screen. In a lobby a **CHAT** button sits under the menu
button: it opens the chat bar above the keyboard and hides the touch controls
until you send or tap the scene. The newest three chat lines (two in landscape)
show above the thumb clusters. A phone here means touch as the primary input with no
hover and a short side of 600px or less.

Phones lay out for the way they are held. In portrait the stick and the thumb
cluster sit in the bottom corners and the camera frames you a little below the
middle, so more of the world shows ahead; in landscape they sit at the left and
right edges, low to mid height, leaving the middle clear, and the camera is
centred. Each orientation starts at its own zoom, and a pinched zoom is kept
for that orientation until the page is reloaded. Turning the phone re-lays out
the controls and re-fits the view at once, without dropping the stick, a held
button or a rune wheel held on **CAST**. The controls keep clear of the notch,
rounded corners and the home indicator, and of browser bars as they slide in
and out.

Phones play like an app. The game starts behind a **TAP TO PLAY** card (on
a first visit, behind the character creator, whose **Create character** does
the same); the tap turns on the sound and goes full screen where the browser
allows it (iPhone Safari does not; add the game to the home screen there
instead). The
page itself never zooms, scrolls, bounces, pulls to refresh or selects text,
and pinching anywhere only zooms the scene. The screen stays awake while you
play. Switching to another app or locking the phone pauses the whole world and
its sound; coming back shows **TAP TO RESUME**, and the world waits until the
tap. The menu's **Full screen** row enters or leaves full screen where the
browser supports it. **Add to Home Screen** installs the game as an app that
opens full screen with a dark status bar.

Tablets get the same thumb cluster and keep the full layout: the panels, the Inventory button and the upper
**− / +** zoom buttons, which work alongside pinch. Touch controls appear only
when touch is the primary input, so touchscreen laptops and narrow desktop
windows keep the keyboard and mouse layout. The scenery builder needs a desktop
or tablet.
