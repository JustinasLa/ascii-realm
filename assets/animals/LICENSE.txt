Animal models
=============

All the models here are in the public domain: the deer, stag, wolf, horse,
rabbit and fish are dedicated to it under CC0 1.0 Universal
(https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), and the pigeon by its
author's own public-domain dedication (below). No attribution is required;
the authors are credited here with thanks.

deer.glb, stag.glb
  "Deer" and "Stag" from the Ultimate Animated Animals pack by Quaternius
  (https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimateanimatedanimals.html), CC0.
  Downloaded as GLB from Poly Pizza:
    https://poly.pizza/m/T6Cs7tmMHJ (Deer)
    https://poly.pizza/m/tQdzbZ1Cmw (Stag)
  Changes: the duplicate "AnimalArmature|" copies of the 13 clips were
  dropped by loading each file with three.js's GLTFLoader and writing it
  back with GLTFExporter (binary). Geometry, skin, materials and clips are
  otherwise as published.

rabbit.glb
  "Rabbit" by Čestmír Dammer (Kelgar project, www.kelgar.org; idea by Rick
  Hoppmann and Keppu), CC0, from OpenGameArt:
    https://opengameart.org/node/51722 (rabbit-FBX.7z)
  Changes: rabbit.fbx was converted to glTF with three.js's FBXLoader and
  GLTFExporter. The FBX's zero-opacity material was made opaque; the body
  keeps its fur texture (rabbit-skinn.png), scaled to 256x256 JPEG; the
  separate fur-shell mesh, its one-frame clip and the "Dying.001" clip were
  left out; the "Armature|" prefix was stripped from the clip names (Basic,
  Guard, Guarding, Jump, Running, Sitting.000, Sitting.001, Dying.000, Dead).

pigeon.glb
  "Animated Bird, Pigeon" by Paul Spooner (dudecon), public domain. The
  author released all his work, past and future, to the public domain on
  2014-08-05 (https://ip.tryop.com: "I [...] hereby release the entirety of
  my intellectual property to the public domain. All ideas which I have
  produced in the past, or will produce in the future, I hereby make
  available for the free and unrestricted use by all people for all
  time."); his site says the release "includes all the stuff on this
  website" (https://peripheralarbor.com, update of 2014-08-10), and his own
  description of this model reads "Actual license is 'Public Domain' see
  details here https://ip.tryop.com/".
  Source: the Blender file on the author's site, downloaded on 2026-10-25:
    https://peripheralarbor.com/bird.blend
    (459,693 bytes, SHA-256
    b44c94cbd1b3dff3a87da63a7f253b7fbc6b80cf3d898292ed56c22930fc4716)
  Changes: exported to glTF with Blender 3.6: only the "rig" armature and
  the "Bird" mesh were kept; the "Pigeon Proportions" action was laid under
  each clip, and "Standing Idle", "Takeoff", "Flapping", "Gliding" and
  "Landing" were baked with visual keying to the deform bones (76 joints),
  the space in "Standing Idle" made an underscore; the texture packed in the
  .blend ("Bird Texture.jpg", 512x512) was scaled to 256x256 JPEG.
  Geometry and animation are otherwise as the author made them.
