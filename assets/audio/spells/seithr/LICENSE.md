# Seithr recorded foley sources

All clips are derived from real field/foley recordings. No synthesized sound is included.
The game clips are mono 48 kHz PCM16 crops of Freesound HQ MP3 previews.
Changes: crop, mono downmix, 22 Hz high-pass/DC removal, short endpoint fades and peak normalization.
No endorsement by the original artists is implied.

- **Ice Fields Moving Rumbling** by **AlBundyX**, [Freesound](https://freesound.org/s/549014/), [CC0-1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Recorded on an ice field under a cardboard box; physical ice rumble and cracks.
- **Ice cubes creaky** by **Salomé_Lubczanski**, [Freesound](https://freesound.org/s/733814/), [CC0-1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Unedited ice cubes recorded with Aquarian H1a hydrophone and Sound Devices Mix-Pre 6 ii.
- **Ice - Lake fractures** by **Vrymaa**, [Freesound](https://freesound.org/s/737896/), [CC0-1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Close recording of frozen lake fractures; no added reverb or echo.
- **Snow crunch 01.WAV** by **luminadii**, [Freesound](https://freesound.org/s/336765/), [CC-BY-4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Snow crunching under feet; cropped individual crush and scuff.
- **wind_forest_08_strong_l_01.wav** by **teadrinker**, [Freesound](https://freesound.org/s/403051/), [CC0-1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Wind through trees recorded with Zoom H4n in Rosendal, Nerikes Kil, Sweden.
- **wind 3** by **ZIP.Creates**, [Freesound](https://freesound.org/s/726314/), [CC0-1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Physical breath blown gently across a Zoom H2n windscreen; recorded foley, not synthesis.

Exact source hashes, crop positions and derived-file hashes are in `sources.json`.
Rebuild with `python tools/render-spell-audio-seithr.py` (numpy, scipy and ffmpeg required).
