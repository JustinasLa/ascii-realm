Adaptation:
- Decode/downmix both recordings to mono at 22050 Hz.
- Apply Butterworth bandpass designs using scipy.signal.butter(4, [...],
  btype='bandpass'): leaf 900-7000 Hz; sole 220-1700 Hz. Filter the full source
  before taking the excerpt. Sole excerpts begin at 0 seconds.
- Leaf grains: 4ms fade in, 25ms initial contact, exponential decay with
  45ms time constant, and 12ms end fade. Normalize the grain peak to 0.62.
- Sole contacts: 5ms fade in, 23ms exponential decay, and 12ms fade to
  silence by 85ms. Peak at most 0.09; energy at most 8% of the leaf grain.
- Mix those two recorded components, normalize final peak to 0.62, and
  export mono 16-bit PCM WAV. Playback adds only short boundary fades.

All six samples replace the earlier Fantozzi SandL/SandR excerpts entirely.
No Fantozzi samples, synthetic noise, liquid sounds, or long scuff tails remain
in these grass footfalls. Other terrain materials are separate.
