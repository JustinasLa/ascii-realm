grass-L1.wav through grass-L3.wav derive from Fantozzi-SandL1.flac
through Fantozzi-SandL3.flac; grass-R1.wav through grass-R3.wav derive
from the corresponding Fantozzi-SandR recordings.

The source pack describes these as suitable for grass and sand.
Adaptation for ASCII Realm: mono 16-bit PCM at 22050 Hz, 80 Hz highpass
to remove low recording rumble. No synthetic squeeze, splash or noise
layers are mixed into grass footfalls.
