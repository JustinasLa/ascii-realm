Adaptation:
- Decode/downmix to mono at 48000 Hz with ffmpeg.
- Filter the full recording with a third-order 450-3800 Hz Butterworth
  bandpass (scipy.signal.butter(..., output='sos') and sosfiltfilt).
- Extract the six 320ms windows above, preserving their recorded envelopes.
- Apply squared-sine boundary fades: 16ms in and 40ms out.
- Balance each excerpt toward RMS 0.038, with amplification capped at 12x.
- Restrain isolated peaks with 0.24 * tanh(signal / 0.24).
- Resample with scipy.signal.resample_poly(147, 320) and export mono
  16-bit PCM at 22050 Hz. Playback adds short boundary fades only.

All grass movement and foot contact in these files comes from this recording.
No kitchen leaf rustles, carpet contacts or generated noise are mixed in.
Files named L/R are variation identifiers; original foot sides are not annotated.
