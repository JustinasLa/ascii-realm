thunder.wav is a 9.5-second excerpt beginning at 21.38 seconds, covering the
recorded thunderclap and rolling tail. Converted to mono 24,000 Hz, 16-bit PCM;
a second-order 35 Hz high-pass removes subsonic rumble, DC is removed, peak is
balanced to 0.80, and a 4 ms entry fade and 1.8-second cosine-squared exit fade
avoid cut clicks. No generated noise or oscillators are added.
