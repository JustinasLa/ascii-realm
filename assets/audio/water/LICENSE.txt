Processing: converted to mono 24,000 Hz, 16-bit PCM. Second-order 55 Hz
high-pass removes subsonic rumble, DC is removed, then levels are balanced to
peaks of 0.55 (skid), 0.60 (wash), 0.65 (wade/lift), 0.78 (splash), 0.85 (big).
4 ms sine-squared entry fades and 45-240 ms cosine-squared exit fades avoid
edit clicks. The natural water displacement, bubbles and returning droplets
remain; no generated noise, compression, looping or reversed audio is added.
